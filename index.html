Support

ShotClean · Last updated November 22, 2026

Need help with ShotClean? Email shotclean.app@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

I deleted screenshots but my storage didn't change

iOS keeps deleted photos in the Recently Deleted album for 30 days. Open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted and choose Delete All to free the space immediately.

The app says no screenshots were found

ShotClean needs access to your photo library. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › ShotClean and choose Full Access.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open ShotClean, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name › Subscriptions › ShotClean, then Cancel Subscription.
