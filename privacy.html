Privacy Policy

ShotClean · Last updated November 22, 2026

ShotClean is built so that your photos never leave your iPhone. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

Data we collect

None. ShotClean does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Your photos

With your permission, ShotClean reads your photo library to find screenshots and similar images. All analysis, including duplicate detection, runs entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Photos are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere. Photos are only deleted when you confirm the deletion in the iOS system dialog, and deleted photos stay in the Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days.

Information stored on your device

The app stores a small counter of how many free deletions you have used. It stays on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

ShotClean does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
