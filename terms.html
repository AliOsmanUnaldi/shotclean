What the app does

ShotClean helps you find and delete screenshots and similar images in your photo library. You decide what is deleted; the app never deletes anything without your confirmation in the iOS system dialog. You are responsible for reviewing items before deleting them.

Free use and ShotClean Pro

A limited number of deletions are free. ShotClean Pro unlocks unlimited deletions and is offered as a weekly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before purchase.
